Bath and current density

Selecting a reference keeps your entered values. Use profile settings replaces the bath label and electrical values; it uses a published point or the lower end of a range. This is an editable reference, not a starting-density recommendation. Missing references stay blank.

Power supply

Operating voltage references can load a voltage setpoint in constant-voltage mode. A constant-current voltage limit must be entered separately. Changing modes clears the voltage field.
